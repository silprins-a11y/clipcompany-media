Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Who we are

Clipcompany Publisher ("the tool") is a private, self-hosted scheduling tool operated by the Clipcompany team ("we", "us") for Clipcompany's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than our own team.

2. What data the tool stores

When we connect a TikTok account through TikTok's Login Kit, the tool stores, on our own server:

  • the access token and refresh token TikTok issues for that account, encrypted at rest, used only to publish our posts and read their publishing status;
  • the account's TikTok open ID, display name and profile picture, used to show which account a post goes to;
  • the videos, captions and settings of the posts we schedule, and the publish status TikTok reports back.

The tool does not read followers, comments, messages, or any data about other TikTok users. It requests only the permissions TikTok shows during authorization: basic profile information and the ability to upload and publish videos to the connected account.

3. How the data is used

Exclusively to schedule and publish our own content to our own connected accounts and to show us whether a post was published. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics about individuals.

4. Where the data lives and who can see it

All data is stored on infrastructure we control. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, other than being sent to TikTok's API when a post is published, as required to provide the function.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile data are kept for as long as the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool deletes its tokens and revokes the tool's access; access can also be revoked from the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Scheduled posts and media can be deleted from the tool at any time.

6. Cookies and tracking on this website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log standard server access data as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about personal data: open an issue on github.com/silprins-a11y/clipcompany-media.
