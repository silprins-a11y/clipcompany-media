The tool is not offered to the public. Only members of the Clipcompany team may sign in, connect accounts, or publish content. No accounts are created for, or on behalf of, third parties.

Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. We may disconnect an account at any time from within the tool, which revokes the tool's access.
