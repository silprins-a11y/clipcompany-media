Clipcompany Publisher

Self-hosted scheduling tool for Clipcompany's own short-form videos.

What it does

Clipcompany produces short entertainment clips for its own channels (TikTok @clippy4483, Instagram clipcompany02, YouTube @Clipcompany02). Clipcompany Publisher is the team's private, self-hosted instance of the open-source scheduler Postiz. The team reviews each clip, schedules it, and the tool publishes it to the connected Clipcompany account at the scheduled time.

Who uses it

Only the Clipcompany team. No third parties can sign up, connect accounts, or post through this tool. The connected TikTok account is owned and operated by the same team that operates the tool.

What gets posted

Only content produced by Clipcompany for Clipcompany's own account. The tool adds no watermarks, logos, or promotional overlays to videos. Privacy level, comment, duet and stitch settings and commercial-content disclosure are chosen explicitly for every post before publishing.

Media

This site also hosts the video files the tool publishes, under this same domain.
